Project Overview

Terratraq uses transfer learning with MobileNetV2 to analyze road imagery and predict the surface condition. Each upload produces a prediction, a confidence score, and a full probability breakdown across all five classes.

Good
Smooth, well-maintained roads
Rough
Uneven, damaged surfaces
Flooded
Roads covered with water
Crack
Roads with surface cracks
Pothole
Roads with visible potholes
Not sure which?
Upload any road photo — we'll classify it for you.
Dataset

Combined dataset from FloodNet, Road Traversing, and Unified Road Defect datasets — split into training, validation and test sets.

41,137 images 30,105 train 5,865 val 5,167 test
Model

MobileNetV2 with transfer learning, fine-tuned on road imagery — 2,299,141 trainable parameters, achieving 89.36% accuracy on the test set.
