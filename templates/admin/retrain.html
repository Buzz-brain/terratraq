Training a CNN requires a GPU and thousands of images, so it runs in Google Colab — not on this server. Once trained, the finished model is uploaded here and goes live immediately (no restart needed).

1
Train in Colab

MobileNetV2 transfer learning on your labeled dataset (e.g. good/, pothole/, crack/, ...). Upload dataset files for Colab via the Manage Dataset page.

2
Export the artifacts

Save model_final.h5 and class_names.pkl, plus confusion_matrix.png and training_history.png for the System Settings page.

3
Download from Colab

Download the exported files to your machine.

4
Upload below

Upload model_final.h5 and class_names.pkl in the "Update CNN Model" panel. The new model activates immediately.
